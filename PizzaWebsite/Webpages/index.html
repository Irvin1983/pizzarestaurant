Welcome to Woodfired Restaurant

At WoodFired Restaurant we specialise in wood fired clay ovens. Almost everything we cook is made in one of our specialist clay ovens, fired by Applewood and Oak all our food is infused with the rich flavours that these wood varieties produce.
