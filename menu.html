Our Menu

Woodfired Toasted Sandwiches

  • Chicken Club
    Three layers of toasted thick-cut sourdough bread, streaky bacon, Cajun marinated chicken, topped with spicy homemade mayonnaise, lettuce, tomato and melted apple smoked cheddar cheese.
  • Bacon, Brie and Cranberry
    Streaky back bacon, melted brie and cranberry sauce all served in toasted thick-cut sourdough bread.
  • Roasted Beetroot and Goat’s Cheese
    Toasted thick-cut sourdough bread, smoked and roasted beetroot topped off with melted goat’s cheese and rocket.
  • Mozzarella, Sun-dried Tomato and Pesto
    Toasted thick-cut sourdough bread, melted mozzarella, sun-dried tomatoes and homemade pesto.
  • Steak and Blue Cheese
    Woodfired sirloin steak sliced marinated in garlic lime and chilli, melted Strathdon blue cheese, topped with an onion relish and gilled peppers, served between two slices of toasted thick-cut sourdough bread.

All sandwiches served with a choice of homemade sweet potato chips, crisps or a salad.
